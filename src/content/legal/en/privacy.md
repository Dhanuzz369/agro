This policy covers goodbizagro.com, the website of GoodBiz Agri, an agricultural export business in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India ("we", "us"). We decide how the personal data described here is used.

Questions or requests: [agro@goodbizglobal.com](mailto:agro@goodbizglobal.com) or +91 88847 99369.

## What we collect

**When you ask for a quote.** The quote form asks for your name, company, email and WhatsApp number, the product, variety and quantity you need, your destination country and port, the Incoterm, and any message. It also records the page and language you used. Each step is saved as you go, so your contact details reach us once you move past the first step, even if you never press Send.

**When you write, call or message us.** We receive whatever you send by email, phone or WhatsApp.

**When you browse.** With your consent where the law requires it, Google Analytics records the pages you open, the site that sent you, your device, browser and approximate location (country and city), and a few actions: sending a quote request and tapping our WhatsApp, email or phone links. Our host, Vercel, keeps short-lived server logs (IP address, browser, time) to run and protect the site, and Vercel Web Analytics and Speed Insights count visits and loading speed without cookies.

We do not ask for payment details, identity documents or anything about your health, beliefs or similar sensitive matters.

## Why we use it

- To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation and, if you go ahead, handle the order. This is a step you ask for before a contract, or our legitimate interest in replying to a business enquiry.
- To keep the business records that Indian tax, customs and export rules require.
- To learn which pages buyers find useful and improve the site. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland this relies on your consent; elsewhere, on our legitimate interest in running a useful website.

We do not sell your data, and we do not use it for advertising.

## Cookies and browser storage

| Name | Set by | What it does | How long |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_ga`, `_ga_XPRPV7VPW5` | Google Analytics | Tells one visit from another | Up to 2 years |
| `googtrans` | Google Translate | Remembers a machine-translated language you chose | Until you close the browser |
| `gb.consent` | This site | Remembers your cookie choice | Until you clear it |
| `gb.lang` | This site | Remembers the language you picked | Until you clear it |
| `gb.suggest.dismissed` | This site | Stops the language suggestion coming back | Until you clear it |

The `gb.` entries are kept in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. In the EU, EEA, UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics sets no cookies until you choose Allow. You can change your answer at any time:

## Who handles it for us

- **Google**: Google Apps Script and Google Sheets store quote requests, Gmail tells us a new one has arrived, Google Analytics measures visits, and Google Translate translates pages into languages we have not translated ourselves.
- **Vercel**: hosts the website.
- **WhatsApp (Meta)**: carries messages you send us there, under WhatsApp's own privacy policy.
- When an enquiry becomes an order, we share what is needed with the people who make the shipment happen, such as suppliers, freight forwarders, customs brokers, inspection laboratories and banks.

## Data sent outside your country

Our team works from India, and Google and Vercel may process data in the United States and other countries. Both offer the European Commission's standard contractual clauses for these transfers.

## How long we keep it

- Quote requests and messages that do not lead to an order: up to 3 years after our last contact, then deleted.
- Orders and the correspondence behind them: as long as Indian tax and export law requires us to keep business records.
- Google Analytics data: up to 14 months.
- Vercel server logs: a short time, as Vercel sets for our plan.

## Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it, and you can withdraw consent at any time without affecting what was done before. Write to [agro@goodbizglobal.com](mailto:agro@goodbizglobal.com) and we will reply within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our answer, you can complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. In India this is the Data Protection Board of India; in the EU, your national supervisory authority; in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

## Children

This is a business-to-business site for importers and traders. It is not meant for anyone under 18, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

## Changes to this policy

When we change how we handle data, we update this page and the date at the top.
